=== Sheet: Stats ===
Term | Description
Player Stats | 
Player stats cannot go below 0 (except HP). | 
Class Level / Weapon Level | Levels determine which abilities an entity has access to. A move can only be used if the user has the required Level.
HP | Health Points starts at the entity's Max HP and is lowered by taking damage. HP cannot exceed Max HP.
When an entity is at 0 or less HP, they are removed from the game.
Max HP | Maximum HP does not change from taking damage, but it can by affected by items and certain effects.
A / ATK | Attack is added to the damage of physical attacks.
M / MAG | Magic is added to the damage of magical attacks.
PD | Physical Defense is subtracted from the damage of incoming physical attacks.
MD | Magical Defense is subtracted from the damage of incoming magical attacks.
EVA | Evasion rounds down. An attack misses if its accuracy roll is less than [the attack's MR] + [the target's EVA].
MP | Movement Points determine how many tiles an entity's Basic Movement action can move over.
MP can also be spent on certain effects. They are refreshed at the start of each turn.
 | 

=== Sheet: Abilities ===
Term | Description
Definitions | 
Move | An ability that isn't classified as Passive (has to be used).
Attack | An ability with a accuracy check (or substitute) and a damage roll.
Frequency | 
Usage Limit | 
If no usage limit is specified, moves may be used any number of times during a game. | 
Once | Can only be used once per game by this entity.
Twice | Can only be used twice per game by this entity.
Thrice | Can only be used three times per game by this entity.
Cooldown | 
Every Turn | Can be used each turn. Once/Twice/Thrice abilities are considered Every Turn if no cooldown is specified.
(If a special source grants more actions than usual, Movement/Standard/Full moves can be used multiple times per turn.)
EoT | Every Other Turn. Cannot be used again for one round (usually the entity's previous turn).
E3T | Every Third Turn. Cannot be used again for two rounds (usually the entity's previous two turns).
Roll & Miss Rate | 
Roll Notation | Attack rolls are written as XdY+Z, where X is the number of dice rolled, Y is the number of base faces, and Z is a flat damage bonus added to the roll.
Miss Rate | MR determines how likely it is for a move to miss, along with EVA.
Damage Type | 
Physical | Adds the user's ATK, and subtracts the target's PD.
Magical | Adds the user's MAG, and subtracts the target's MD.
Action Type | 
Movement | One Movement action can be used per turn (before any Standard actions are used, unless the user is a Juggernaut). The Basic Movement action lets entities spend MP to move that many tiles. Entities cannot move diagonally.
Standard | One Standard action may be used every turn. If an entity can no longer use any Standard actions, their turn ends.
Full | Full actions occupy both an entity's Movement and Standard action.
Swift | One Swift action can be used per turn. They cannot be used alongside Free actions.
Free | Any amount of Free actions can be used per turn, but each Free action can only be used once per turn.
They cannot be used alongside Swift actions.
Trigger | Each Trigger action can be used once per turn, and can be used with Free or Swift actions.
Some Triggers have a certain condition when they can be used (stated in the move).
Reaction | Reactions can be used when their condition is fulfilled (stated in the move). Reactions become invalid after 30 seconds. Each player cannot use more than one Reaction in response to an action, and they cannot react to a Reaction. If used in response to an attack, the Reaction is activated after damage is rolled (or another trigger specified in the description), but effects are resolved before damage is dealt, with any damage from the Reaction being dealt afterwards. (Players may declare Reactions without proper move format to use it.)
Amount | 
1.0 | Targets one entity.
2, 3, etc. | May target up to X entities.
AoE | Targets all valid entities within range. AoE follows Range Rule, unless the obstructions are also targets. Their length cannot be increased/decreased, unless specfied otherwise. (Splash is AoE.)
Group | 
Moves only target entities in the specified group(s). | 
Any | Any entity.
Self | The user of the ability.
Ally | A member of the user's team (except the user). The user can also target foes if both players consent.
Foe | An entity that is not the user or an ally.
Tile | A tile coordinate, rather than an entity. Follows Ice Clause.
Range | 
Ranges follow Range Rule: Attacks travel in a straight line from you to your target(s) and cannot be obstructed.
(Global ignores Range Rule. Melee and Homing have special rules.)
**For targeting and Reactions, attacks that exclusively target entities in Melee range are treated (but not classified) as a Melee attack. | 
Melee | Targets any of the 8 tiles adjacent to the user. Unlike most ranges, it can target diagonally through two obstructions if at least one of them is an entity. A Melee ability that gains range becomes Range X but is still classified as Melee, and Melee range cannot be decreased.
Range X | Targets up to X tiles away from the user (diagonals count as two tiles).
Homing X | Similar to Range X, but is able to curve around tiles and obstructions following the grid.
Line X | Targets up to X tiles away in one of 8 directions: a cardinal line, or X/2 tiles diagonally (rounded up). Cannot target through obstructed Corners.
Global | Targets any entity anywhere on the map, ignoring Range Rule. Can be AoE.
AoE Ranges | 
The length of AoE ranges cannot be increased/decreased, unless specified otherwise. | 
Burst X | Targets in a square area, X tiles out from each side of the user.
Star X | Targets all tiles of Range X.
Pierce X | Targets all tiles in one direction of Line X (cardinally or diagonally). Cannot target through obstructed Corners.
Beam X | Hits an area three tiles wide in front of the user and X tiles deep.
Cone X | Hits in a triangle area in front of the user in a cardinal direction. Cone 1 is the 3 spaces in front of the user in melee, Cone 2 is those plus the 5 spaces behind them, etc.
Other Attributes | 
Advantage | Rolls with Advantage roll each dice twice, then only user the higher roll of each individual pair of dice. (Roll using %roll XdY+Z adv.) This is a positive dice and damage factor.
Disadvantage | Rolls with Disadvantage roll each dice twice, then discard (don't use) the higher roll of each individual pair of dice. (Roll using %roll XdY+Z disadv.) This is a negative dice and damage factor.
Delay | Delay moves have their damage and effects delayed for a specified number of rounds, hitting at the start of the user's turn. Damage is rolled when the move is used, with damage altering effects applied on use.
Bomb Dice | The top 25% of potential dice faces (d2 and above) (d6: 5+, d8: 7+, d10: 8+, etc.) spawn an extra dice roll. Extra dice rolls generated this way are also considered bomb dice. (Roll using %roll XdY+Z bomb.) This is a positive dice and damage factor.
Inverse Bomb Dice | The top 25% of potential dice faces (d2 and above) (d6: 5+, d8: 7+, d10: 8+, etc.) create an extra dice roll that subtract from the total roll instead of adding. (Roll using %roll XdY+Z inversebomb.) This is a negative dice and damage factor.
Multi-Hit | The attack has multiple hits. Roll accuracy for each individual hit and calculate damage accordingly. Only the first hit adds ATK/MAG and subtracts DEF. Outside effects that trigger based on accuracy only trigger once.
Pull X | The target is moved X tiles towards the user in a straight line, following the "Range" range type.
(Some angles pull targets across the corners of four tiles; targets are fully pulled through unobstructed corners even if they would only be pulled 1 more tile.)
Push X | The target is moved X tiles away from the user in a straight line, following the "Range" range type.
(Some angles push targets across the corners of four tiles; targets are fully pushed through unobstructed corners even if they would only be pushed 1 more tile.)
Splash X | If the move targets a valid entity, it becomes AoE and targets all valid entities within Burst X of the original entity. An attack hits/misses on all targets if it hit/misses the original target. Range modifiers only affect the initial range.

=== Sheet: Map ===
Term | Description
Definitions | 
Range Rule | Abilities of range types that follow Range Rule cannot be used if, when drawing a straight line from the center of the user's to the target's tile, that line intersects with any obstruction.
Obstructions | Stop/Bone, Ice, Stone, and Hearth tiles, as well as foes, are considered obstructions. Unless specified otherwise, obstructions may not be moved through or targetted through, and these tiles cannot be moved nor replaced by other tiles.
Tile-Changing Effects | Tiles that are changed by effects revert to Normal tiles when the effect ends.
Tiles | 
Air | Ranged attacks that fully pass through Air tiles lose 1 range.
Forest | Entities on Forest tiles have +5 Physical Defense, but -1 EVA against Physical attacks.
Water | Entities on Water tiles have +5 Magical Defense, but -1 EVA against Magical attacks.
Stop | Stop tiles cannot be attacked through nor moved through, and turns cannot be ended on them. They also cannot be moved nor replaced by other tiles.
Bone | Bone tiles are the same as Stop tiles.
Broken | Broken tiles cannot be moved through, and turns cannot be ended on them.
Ice | Ice tiles have the properties of Stop tiles. However, they can be targeted by (non-Global) attacks, and break if that attack deals 15+ damage. Ice Clause: Effects do not interact with nor apply to Ice tiles. (Effects outside of the "Effects" column still apply, such as "X", Multihit, and Splash.)
Lava | Entities take 20 damage when entering Lava tiles, and take 30 damage when they end their turn on Lava.
Sticky | Sticky tiles take 1 more MP than normal (usually 2) to enter.
Boost | Boost tiles take 1 less MP than normal (usually 0) to enter. Entities cannot enter Boost tiles if they have 0 MP left.
 | 

=== Sheet: Effects ===
Term | Description
Statuses | 
Damaging statuses deal damage just before the affected entity's turn. They do not stack together, even if they are the same type. If an entity currently has a damaging status, they cannot be affected by a new one unless the new one is unremovable and the old one isn’t, in which case the new status overrides the old one. A damaging status is typically denoted with X (Status)/Y, which means the status deals X damage and has a duration of Y rounds. A Non-Damaging Status is typically denoted with (Status)/Y, which means the status has a duration of Y rounds. Reapplying a non damaging status restarts the status with its new specified duration. | 
Damaging Statuses | 
Bleed | Reduces damage by 5.
Burn | Reduces accuracy by 2.
Curse | Reduces Defence by 4.
Poison | Reduces Evasions by 2.
Non-Damaging Statuses | 
Confusion | If the affected player rolls 16+ for accuracy while under the effects of Confusion, the user takes their higher offensive stat (ATK/MAG) in damage. This damage cannot be taken more than once for multi-hit moves.
Cripple | Cripple is denoted with X Cripple/Y. The affected player loses X HP for every tile they willingly move while affected. This effect lasts for Y rounds.
Root | The affected player is unable to use their movement action while affected.
Seal | The affected player can only use Basic Abilities other than Attack of Opportunity, unless the move states it can be used while sealed. Passives are unaffected.
Slow | The affected player can only move up to half their MP stat, rounded up.
Stun | The affected player is unable to use any action, including reactions, until the effect wears off. Self-inflicted stun cannot be removed or suppressed in any way.
Other Effects | 
Shield | Whenever a player with a Shield would take damage, the Shield does instead until it runs out of HP or its duration ends. While a player has a shield, abilities that target them cannot inflict non-damaging statuses. This does not remove current ones.
Buff / Debuff | Buffs are always underlined and Debuffs are always italicised in ability effects.
(In general, a Buff or Debuff is a finite duration effect that increases or decreases an entity's stats, accuracy, or damage.)
 | 

=== Sheet: Other ===
Term | Description
Definitions | 
Critical Hit | An accuracy roll of 20+ is a Critical Hit, which doubles the amount of base dice on the damage roll. This is not affected by ±ACC effects, but instead ±CR. (Accuracy rolls with different dice size still crits on 20+.)
Critical Miss | An accuracy roll of 1 is always a miss, unless specified otherwise. This is not affected by positive or negative accuracy modifiers.
Critical Range (CR) | For each point of Critical Range, the accuracy roll required to land a critical hit is reduced by 1. Negative CR instead increases the accuracy roll needed for a Critical Miss.
Entity | Anything that is targettable under normal circumstances (e.g players and monsters).
Round | Cooldowns and some effects last X number of rounds. Their "rounds" are based on which entity's turn the effects were applied during. At the end of that entity's future turns, X decreases by 1; the Cooldown/effect ends when X reaches 0.
Outside Factors | Effects that aren't written in the move itself are considered outside factors. This does not include stat changes.
Teleport | The user’s position changes from their current tile to the target tile. The user ignores intermediate tiles and players and is not considered to have moved over them. (Teleport movement ignores AoO.)
Unremovable | Unremovable effects cannot be suppressed in any way (including having an existing normal status; the new status will override the previous.) In addition to being unsuppressible, unremovable effects cannot be changed in any way (unless specified otherwise), excluding status damage. This includes moves immune to outside factors.
Clarifications | 
Accuracy | Effects based on accuracy rolls are affected by accuracy modifiers. The only exception of this are critical hits, which cannot be gained or lost through accuracy modifiers.
Dice Modifiers | Changes to base dice through Crits or Jugg are NOT considered dice modifiers.
Corners | Sometimes, players or ranges go through the corner of four tiles: Push/Pull, Line X, etc. If the two tiles (that the path doesn't cross) share a tile property, then the corner also has that property. 2+ obstructions also obstruct the corner (unless an AoE targets one/both of them).
Damage Order | When both % based effects (such as Radiance) and flat damage modifiers (such as bleed, TM, etc.) affect the same move, the addition/subtraction damage modifiers occur BEFORE the multiplication % effect.
End of Game | A 1v1 game ends when 1 player dies (post-death effects are not resolved). This does not apply to other modes, where effects still take place after a game over condition is met, until that turn ends. (The game continues if the effects cause game over conditions to fail.)
Effect Order | Unless specified otherwise, the damage and effects of attacks/reactions occur in the following order:
1. Attack Accuracy -> 2. Attack Damage Roll -> 3. Reaction Accuracy -> 4. Reaction Damage Roll ->
5. Reaction Effects -> 6. Attack Damage Dealt -> 7. Reaction Damage Dealt -> 8. Attack Effects.
Ice Clause | Effects do not interact with nor apply to Ice tiles. (Effects outside of the "Effects" column still apply, such as "X", Multihit, and Splash.)
Pending Effects | Pending effects, i. e. effects that rely on their target interacting with the user in some way on their turn (e. g. Provoke), are negated if the user or the target (whichever is applicable) is dead at the start of the target's turn.
Secondary Effects | Secondary effects happen after damage is rolled, unless specified.
Recoil / Reaction | Recoil and Reaction damage comes AFTER the original move's damage is dealt. This means that in 1v1s, if a user dies from one of these but kills another player, they win.
Sacrifice | All sacrifice and spending based effects resolve before accuracy is rolled, unless specified otherwise.
Removal of Modifiers | The removal of positive modifiers (e. g. Shock and Awe) is not a negative outside factor.
Rewinding Time | If an event of any kind (e.g. Passive, Item) was forgotten or not taken into account due to a mistake, but it doesn't impact other parts of the game significantly, it can retroactively take place. Otherwise, the host may choose to either rewind time back to when the event would've happened (if not too many moves have been used since then) and continue from there or to continue the game with the event never happening. In case of a dispute, auths can make a final decision.
 |
